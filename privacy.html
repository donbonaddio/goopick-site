Privacy Policy

GOOPICK · Last updated October 2026

GOOPICK collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The game runs entirely on your device and works fully offline.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. GOOPICK does not ask you to sign in, and it never sends your data to us or to any third party.

Your game data

Your progress, collection, and settings are saved only on your own device using Apple’s standard on-device storage. We have no access to it. Deleting the app removes it.

Children

GOOPICK is suitable for all ages and, because it collects no data at all, it does not knowingly or unknowingly gather information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
